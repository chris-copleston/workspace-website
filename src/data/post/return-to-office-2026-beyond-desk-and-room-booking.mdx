The return to office (RTO) is accelerating across the UK, USA, Canada and Brazil. But getting employees back into the office is only half the challenge. Organisations must provide the right spaces, resources and experiences while managing attendance, capacity, operational costs and increasingly diverse workplace policies.

Employees want flexibility, convenience and the ability to organise their working day without unnecessary friction. Facilities Managers need control, visibility and efficient resource allocation. Workplace consultants need reliable utilisation data to develop effective workplace strategies. Architects need to design adaptable spaces that accommodate changing patterns of work.

The common requirement is a workplace that is **flexible by design, easy to use and intelligent enough to evolve**.

## Why Traditional Booking Systems Are No Longer Enough

Many workplace platforms were originally designed around a relatively simple requirement: booking a meeting room or reserving a desk.

Today's enterprise workplace is considerably more complex.

- **Desk booking alone** cannot manage team attendance, changing allocation policies, lockers, parking or the wider employee experience.
- **Room booking alone** cannot coordinate centralised executive support, VIP requirements, confidential meetings, visitor needs and wider workplace resources.
- **Disconnected applications** create unnecessary complexity for employees and fragmented data for Facilities teams.
- **Basic reporting** tells organisations what happened, but provides limited insight into future demand, space efficiency and changing attendance patterns.

For [banking](/solutions/banking), financial services and [legal](/solutions/legal) organisations, where security, confidentiality, executive support and operational control are particularly important, these limitations become even more significant.

**The answer isn't another booking application. It's a connected workplace management platform.**

## RTO Is a Global Trend, With Different Market Pressures

A snapshot of recent research illustrates how workplace expectations are evolving:

| Market | Key indicator |
|--------|---------------|
| 🇬🇧 **UK** | 53% of employees feel pressure to spend more time in the workplace. *(CIPD)* |
| 🇺🇸 **USA** | Financial services and legal offices in Manhattan averaged 62% of pre-pandemic attendance levels in March 2025. *(Partnership for New York City)* |
| 🇨🇦 **Canada** | 78.8% of workers worked exclusively outside their homes in May 2026; hybrid working remained around 10%. *(Statistics Canada)* |
| 🇧🇷 **Brazil** | 68% of surveyed workers were predominantly office-based in McKinsey's October 2024 research, double the previous sample's 34%. *(McKinsey)* |

*Different surveys and populations; figures are directional indicators, not direct country comparisons.*

The message is consistent: organisations need to **manage attendance more intelligently**, not simply provide more desks.
